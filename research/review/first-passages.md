# First Tibetan passages: a small collection trial

Start with **three short passages** to check that collecting and reviewing material is practical. This is an intake trial before the planned 10–20-passage development screen and timed ten-example review pilot. It does not establish Tibetan model quality or select a model.

The intended readers are adults in Tibet who prefer clear, everyday written Tibetan. Use a writing style you know well, and note unfamiliar terms or regional wording. A coordinator should explain these instructions in the contributor's preferred language; this English document is not a reviewed Tibetan script.

The project owner's initial recruitment community is Lhasa. Use the [Lhasa collection brief](lhasa-first-collection.md) for the current first assignment; participant availability and the exact written register still need confirmation.

## For the author

Copy [the plain-text template](first-passages-template.txt) into a local document or chat with the project coordinator. Fill one copy for each passage. No JSON, IDs or hashes need to be edited.

1. Write about three to six sentences in Tibetan, using ordinary, nonclinical facts: a library's opening hours, a list of objects and their counts, or a fictional travel notice. A clearly labelled fictional scenario is fine. Avoid medical advice and personal information.
2. Prefer your own original writing. If adapting existing material, identify the source and the permission or licence that allows its use. Public availability alone does not establish permission. Leave uncertain rights unresolved.
3. Add four questions in Tibetan: one directly answerable, one checking a number or time, one checking a negative statement or condition, and one whose answer is absent from the passage. Include a negative statement or condition in the passage so that question has something specific to test.
4. Put proposed answers in the separate answer section. For each, point to the supporting words. For the unanswerable question, propose a response that acknowledges the missing information; do not fill the gap using outside knowledge.
5. Record permission for human review and local model evaluation separately. A blank or “unresolved” is not permission. Training, external services and publication are not part of this request.

Use a pseudonym. Do not add names, contact details, personal lab reports or medical histories. Record whether any writing or editing tool was used; do not present machine-generated text as independent human writing. Chinese equivalents can be prepared later with bilingual review; do not use automatic translation as approved parallel material.

## For the coordinator

- Keep completed material outside the repository, in the private local study folder. Preserve the original submission exactly; save corrections as new versions rather than silently changing spelling, spacing or punctuation.
- Explain how the contributor can ask you to correct or withdraw material. Record the permission date, scope and restrictions separately from model exports. Do not promise to undo uses that have already occurred.
- Map the submission into versioned source, example and permission records. Resolve rights before use. Keep the proposed answers pending until independently reviewed; the author's submission is not approval.
- Arrange two independent language reviewers who did not write or co-write the passage, questions or proposed answers. Preserve their separate judgments before a non-author adjudicator records the outcome. Record time and unresolved issues. Use the established [reviewer instructions](reviewer-instructions.md) for formal assignments.
- Select the community/task and record success criteria before scoring models. Choose ten examples for the timed pilot only after their required source and answer reviews are complete. Keep the remaining collection plan proportionate to measured review capacity.
- Any Chinese passage, question and reference answer needs separately recorded rights and bilingual equivalence review before a paired comparison. A same-topic paraphrase is not automatically equivalent.
- These passages are being proposed for a development screen. Keep their grouping and model exposure history; do not later treat them as untouched test material. Training requires a separate permission decision and eligibility check, not reuse by default.

The template collects drafts and permission decisions. It does not replace the versioned research records, independent review packets or adjudication workflow. No human ratings or approvals have been supplied by this kit.
