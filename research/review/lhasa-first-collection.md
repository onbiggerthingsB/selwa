# First collection with Lhasa speakers

The project owner identified **Lhasa** as the community from which reviewers can be recruited. Our working target is clear everyday written Tibetan for adults. The speakers will help confirm the vocabulary and written register; location alone does not settle those choices. Recruitment, permissions and reviews have not yet been recorded.

This first collection checks whether the writing and review process is practical before the model comparison. It uses short nonclinical passages. The longer-term nutrition/health project remains separate from this first comprehension exercise.

## People and first assignment

Arrange **one writer and two other reviewers**. Reviewers must not have written or helped write the passage, questions or proposed answers they assess. The coordinator can organize the files and record time. After independent reviews are preserved, a non-author reviewer can record a separate adjudication with that overlap disclosed; a fourth person is not required for this initial language exercise.

Give the writer the [plain-text collection template](first-passages-template.txt) and this assignment:

> Please write three original short Tibetan passages, each about three to six sentences, in wording that adults you know in Lhasa would find clear. You can use a fictional everyday notice, shop opening hours, a list of objects and counts, or another familiar nonclinical situation. Label invented situations as fictional. Include a number or time and a negative statement or condition.
>
> For each passage, add four Tibetan questions: one about a directly stated fact, one about a number or time, one about a negative statement or condition, and one asking for information the passage does not give. For the first three, copy the exact answer words from the passage into the separate proposed-answer section. For the fourth, explain what information is absent and propose how to say that; the reviewers will help agree on one consistent no-answer response.
>
> Flag uncertain wording and record whether another person or a writing tool helped. Decide separately whether you permit private human review and local model evaluation. Leave unresolved permission questions unresolved. This first assignment does not request training or publication rights.

The coordinator should explain this brief in each person's preferred language. It is an English coordination draft, not a reviewed Tibetan script. Use pseudonyms in the material and keep completed submissions and permission records in the private study folder.

## Independent review

Each reviewer first assesses the same passage, questions and proposed answers independently, using the formal source-review packet/form. Ask whether the writing is natural, the question has a clear meaning, the proposed answer is supported, and numbers or negation have changed. Check that the fourth answer cannot be recovered from the supplied passage. Record active review time, uncertainties and proposed corrections.

Keep both original judgments before discussing disagreements. Corrected text becomes a new version requiring review. The author's proposed answers and a conversation between reviewers do not replace independent approval records. See the [reviewer instructions](reviewer-instructions.md) for the rubric and separate adjudication.

**Three passages produce twelve proposed question examples.** The later agreement pilot covers ten model-output cases; those are a different unit and can include outputs from more than one model on the same example. No model training or comparison starts merely because these drafts exist.
